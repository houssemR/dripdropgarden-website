What we care about

Games you can hand to your kids

Every GuGo game is made to be safe, fair and fun for the whole family.

🧸

Made for families

Bright, gentle and easy to learn, with a Kids land made for the youngest players. No chat, no accounts and no way for strangers to reach your child.

🛡️

Child-safe ads

Our games ask the player's age once. Younger players only see ads suitable for children, never based on their behaviour, and the Kids lands have no ads at all.

💛

Fair to play

Both games are free. Everything in the shop says what's inside, and a grown-up check comes before any purchase by a child.
