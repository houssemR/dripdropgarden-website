Drip Drop Garden

Catch the rain, grow a garden!

Hold a glass watering can under the sky, catch the clean raindrops, dodge the junk that cheeky garden friends throw, and pour fresh water into your flower pot. The cleaner the water, the more beautiful the bloom.

The game

Easy to play, fun to master

Slide your finger to move the can. Catch drops in a row for combos and Fever, grab golden drops, and smash junk before it lands.

🌸

A world of gardens

From the Sunny Meadow to the Old Town, the Seaside Village, the Emerald Jungle, the Sunny Dunes, the Bubble Reef and the Frosty Peaks: 50 levels and a grumpy boss in every garden.

📅

A new garden every month

The Monthly garden has a new level every day, with apples to pick, gift fruits, star chests and a hidden friend to find.

🧸

Gardens for little ones

The Kids gardens are slow, gentle and full of cute friends, made for the youngest players.

🌦️

Every kind of weather

Sunrises, sunsets, starry nights, storms and snow: every garden has its own sky.

🎒

Collect everything

Characters, pots, watering cans, trails and butterflies. Spin the free daily wheel, fill the sticker album and earn badges.

🏆

A season every month

Daily and monthly challenges, season rewards, a daily streak and bonus rewards for keen gardeners.

Garden friends

Meet the gang

Henrietta the hen waters your garden, and dozens of friends join her along the way.

For the whole family

Made with kids in mind

  • ✓
    Child-safe adsThe game asks your age once. Younger players only see ads suitable for children, never based on their behaviour, and the Kids gardens have no ads at all.
  • ✓
    No surprise purchasesEverything you can buy shows exactly what you get. Boxes and spins are only won by playing, never sold.
  • ✓
    A grown-up checkBefore a child can buy anything, the game asks a question only a grown-up should answer.
  • ✓
    No chat, no accountsNothing to sign up for and no way for strangers to talk to your child.
👑

Premium

Drip Drop Garden is free to play. Premium, monthly or yearly, removes all ads, unlocks every season's premium rewards and gives video rewards without watching. It renews until you cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.
