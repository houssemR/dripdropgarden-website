Privacy Policy

Orbit Smash - last updated 20 October 2026

Orbit Smash ("the game") is made by GuGo Studios ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is made for everyone, including children. It has no accounts, no chat, and no way for players to share anything with each other.

What we ask you

The first time the game starts it asks how old you are. Your answer stays on your device (and in your own cloud save, see below). We use it only to choose which ads may be shown and to ask a grown-up before a child buys anything. We do not send your age to anyone.

Your game progress

Your progress (stars, coins, gems, your hammers and guardians, and your settings) is saved on your device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or use Game Center and iCloud (iPhone and iPad), the game also keeps a copy of your progress in that service, so you can continue on another device. That copy is stored by Google or Apple under your own account. Signing in is optional; the whole game works without it.

Downloading regions

Some regions of space and each month of the Monthly land are downloaded while you play, from our content server (run by Cloudflare). Like any website, the server receives your device's IP address to send the files. We do not use it to identify you.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which pay for the free game (the Premium Pass removes them all): an ad between levels now and then (never in the Kids land, and never in your first levels), and videos you choose to watch for a reward.

  • Players under 13: ads are requested as child-directed, are not personalised, and are limited to content rated for general audiences. The advertising identifier is not used for them.
  • Players 13 to 15: ads are not personalised.
  • Players 16 and over: ads may be personalised if you agree. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Google's consent form asks you first, and you can change your choice any time under Settings > Ads privacy choices.

Google may collect device information such as the advertising identifier, IP address and app activity to show ads and measure them. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

You can buy gem packs, boxes and the Premium Pass (a monthly or yearly subscription with no ads). Each box shows the gems, coins and XP it holds and what kind of surprise is inside (a hammer upgrade, a new hammer or a new guardian); the surprise is always the next one in a fixed order, never random. Payments are made through Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details. To deliver purchases, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (what was bought and when) from the store. Before a child buys anything, the game asks a question only a grown-up should answer.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your email, phone number, address or location.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not show ads to children that are based on their behaviour.

Children

We follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy. A parent or guardian who wants their child's game data deleted can uninstall the game (this deletes everything on the device) and remove the saved game from Google Play Games, Game Center or iCloud, or contact us below.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on the device. Your cloud copy can be deleted in your Google Play Games, Game Center or iCloud settings. To ask us to delete purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at its top.
