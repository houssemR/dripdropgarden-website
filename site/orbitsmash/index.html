The game

Touch, swing, SMASH!

Touch anywhere around the Earth and the hammer swings there. A gentle touch blocks, a fast swing smashes, and an even faster one makes a MEGA hit!

🌍

A journey across space

49 levels across 7 regions, from Meteor Meadow to the Galaxy Core. Every region has its own Earth, music and weather, and a boss waiting at the end.

👾

Silly aliens, big bosses

Snowball yetis, lava imps, crystal bats, spore puffs, spark bots and blinking void imps. Then the Rock King, the Frost Titan, the Mothership and more.

⚡

Waves, combos and HYPER

Levels play in waves and the music speeds up with each one. Chain smashes into combos, go HYPER, and tap the glowing Earth for a SUPERNOVA that blasts everything away.

🔨

Hammers and guardians

Upgrade your hammer's power, size and swing, collect 11 hammer skins and build a team of 12 cute guardians: Moon Buddy, Laser Bot, Night Owl, Star Whale and more.

📅

A level every day

The Monthly map has a new level every day. Spin the free daily wheel, keep your streak, take on challenges, fill a season of rewards and collect badges.

🧸

A Kids land

Gentle levels for the littlest players, and a guided first level that teaches everyone how to swing.

Free to play

Orbit Smash is free to play, with optional in-app purchases. The Premium Pass, monthly or yearly, is optional too: it removes all the ads, unlocks every season's premium rewards and gives the video rewards without watching. It renews until you cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.
