Privacy Policy

Drip Drop Garden - last updated 10 October 2026

Drip Drop Garden ("the game") is made by Drip Drop Garden ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is made for everyone, including children. It has no accounts, no chat, and no way for players to share anything with each other.

What we ask you

The first time the game starts it asks how old you are. Your answer stays on your device (and in your own cloud save, see below). We use it only to choose which ads may be shown and to ask a grown-up before anything is bought. We do not send your age to anyone.

Your game progress

Your progress (stars, coins, gems, the things you collected, your settings and the name you type in the game) is saved on your device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iPhone and iPad), the game also keeps a copy of your progress in that service's saved games, so you can continue on another device. That copy is stored by Google or Apple under your own account. Signing in is optional; the whole game works without it.

Downloading gardens

Some gardens are downloaded while you play, from our content server (run by Cloudflare). Like any website, the server receives your device's IP address to send the files. We do not use it to identify you.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which pay for the free game. Ads are never shown in the Kids gardens, and never to players with Premium.

  • Players under 13: ads are requested as child-directed, are not personalised, and are limited to content rated for general audiences. The advertising identifier is not used for them.
  • Players 13 to 15: ads are not personalised.
  • Players 16 and over: ads may be personalised if you agree. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Google's consent form asks you first, and you can change your choice any time under Settings > Ads privacy choices.

Google may collect device information such as the advertising identifier, IP address and app activity to show ads and measure them. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

You can buy gems, the Starter Bag and Premium (monthly or yearly). Payments are made through Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details. To make purchases work across devices and restore them, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (what was bought and when) from the store. Before a child buys anything, the game asks a question only a grown-up should answer.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your email, phone number, address or location.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not show ads to children that are based on their behaviour.

Children

We follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy. A parent or guardian who wants their child's game data deleted can uninstall the game (this deletes everything on the device) and remove the saved game from Google Play Games or Game Center, or contact us below.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on the device. Your cloud copy can be deleted in your Google Play Games or Game Center settings. To ask us to delete purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at its top.
